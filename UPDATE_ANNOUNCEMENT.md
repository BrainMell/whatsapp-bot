  📋 WHAT'S NEW

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚔️  BOSS SPLASH SCREEN
   Every boss now gets a cinematic intro image before combat.
   Tier-colored backgrounds, boss sprite, name, and flavor text.
   S-rank red, SS purple, SSS gold, Trial blue, Dragon orange, Raid cyan.

💰  ECONOMY REBALANCE
   • S/SS/SSS completion gold cut 60% (was way too generous)
   • Boss gold rewards cut 50% across S+
   • Per-dungeon gold cap: F-A=500K, S=1M, SS=2M, SSS=3M
   • Weekly wealth tax: 1% on bank over 10M, 2% over 50M
   • Crafting now costs Zeni for high-tier items (Rare=5K, Epic=25K, Legendary=100K, Mythic=500K)

🏰  GUILD SYSTEM 2.0
   • Archetype perks now WORK (were flavor text before):
     - ADVENTURER: +15% XP from dungeons
     - MERCHANT: +10% gold + 10% sell value
     - RESEARCH: -10% crafting material cost
   • Building perks now WORK:
     - Hall: +5 member cap per level (base 20, max 45)
     - Training: +5% XP per level
     - Treasury: +10% gold per level + daily bank interest
   • Guild levels unlock perks: L2=+5% XP, L3=+5% gold, L5=bank interest, L7=+1 SP on rank-up
   • 4-tier role system: Leader → Officer → Member → Recruit
   • Guild bank loans (borrow up to 10%, 7-day repayment, auto-deduct if overdue)
   • Guild donations (earn guild XP for contributing Zeni)
   • Custom guild emblems (emoji + hex color)
   • Leaderboard now ranks by level + XP (was just mini-game wins)

💎  RUNE SYSTEM
   Socket runes into skills to customize your build!
   • 6 rune types: Power, Efficiency, Spread, Focus, Endurance, Pierce
   • 3 tiers: Lesser, Normal, Greater
   • Slot capacity: Starter=0, Evolved=1, Ascended=2, Ultimate=3
   • Drops from S+ bosses (S=10%, SS=15%, SSS=25%, Dragon=20%)
   • Tradeable on the market (.g rune sell/buy/market)
   • Rune Removal Scrolls (rare drop) for safe removal

🕳️  THE ABYSS — ENDLESS DUNGEON
   Procedural floors that get harder forever. How deep can you go?
   • Floors 1-10: F→A rank enemies
   • Floors 11-20: S rank, mini-bosses every 3rd floor
   • Floors 21-49: SS+ rank, every floor is a boss
   • Floors 50+: SSS rank, brutal
   • Floor 100: The Abyssal God (final boss)
   • Death = lose 90% of run loot | Retreat = keep 100%
   • 12h cooldown between runs, level 20+ required
   • Weekly leaderboard resets every Monday
   • Rune drops on floor 21+ bosses

⚔️  WEEKLY AVATAR RAID
   Server-wide raid where ALL players merge into "The Avatar"!
   • Join the raid → your class + stats merge with all other participants
   • Avatar class = most common class among participants
   • Avatar stats = aggregated + scaled by sqrt(player count)
   • 5 skills from top 5 contributing classes
   • Each round: 60s voting window, vote for which skill the Avatar uses
   • Most-voted skill executes, boss attacks back
   • Continues until boss dies (win) or all attackers dead (loss)
   • 4-boss rotation: Elder Chaos → Void Titan → Abyssal God → Ancient Dragon
   • Boss HP scales with sqrt(active player count)
   • Rewards: Top 3 = 500K XP + Greater Rune, Top 10 = 200K XP + Normal Rune

💰  PVP BOUNTY SYSTEM
   Place Zeni bounties on other players. Hunt them via PvP.
   • Min 100K, Max 50M Zeni per bounty
   • Max 3 active bounties per target (no pile-on)
   • 24h cooldown between placements
   • 7-day expiry with Zeni refund
   • Hunter fee: 5% to hunter's guild treasury
   • Failed hunt: hunter pays 10% penalty to target
   • Targets with bounties CANNOT deposit to bank (forces wallet carry = risk)
   • Auto-claim on PvP win — beat the target, collect the bounty

⚔️  MULTI-EVENT GUILD WARS
   Weekly guild competition with 4 rotating event types!
   • Week 1: CHAMPION TOURNAMENT — 1v1 PvP bracket between guild champions
   • Week 2: GUARDIAN CLASH — 3v3 team PvP between top guilds
   • Week 3: MONSTER HUNT — PvE race (boss kills + Abyss + dungeons)
   • Week 4: STRONGHOLD SIEGE — defend virtual strongholds (activity + RNG)
   • Points from: dungeon clears, boss kills, PvP wins, raid participation, Abyss runs
   • Rewards: 1st = 5M Zeni + 10% buff, 2nd-3rd = 2M + 5% buff, 4th-8th = 500K

🧠  SMARTER MOB AI
   Regular mobs are no longer HP sponges!
   • Low HP (below 30%): mobs use heals, buffs, shields
   • Critical HP (below 15%) + alone: mobs may FLEE (50% success rate)
   • Mid HP (30-60%): mobs use CC and debuffs strategically
   • Bosses phase-shift at 50% and 25% HP with enrage buffs

     Wealth tax runs every Monday 00:00 UTC.

  2. GUILD PERKS: Existing guilds now have WORKING perks. ADVENTURER
     guilds get +15% XP, MERCHANT +10% gold, RESEARCH -10% craft costs.
     This is a significant buff — players will feel it immediately.

  3. RAID SCHEDULER: The raid auto-spawns on boot if missing for the

     /api/combat/splash endpoint. Splash is non-fatal — if Go service
     hasn't been updated, combat proceeds without the splash image.

  6. DATABASE MIGRATION: New schema fields (inventorySlots, undeadKills,
     kills on User; motto, title, buildings, emblem, loans, warPoints on
     Guild) are all backwards-compatible. Existing documents auto-migrate
     on next access. No manual migration needed.
